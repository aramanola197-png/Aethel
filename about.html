About Aethel

Specialist representation for the automotive creator community.

Our Story

Aethel was founded on a simple observation: the automotive content space is full of genuine expertise, yet brands and creators often struggle to find the right partners. Too many campaigns chase reach over relevance. Too many creators are left negotiating without proper support.

We exist to change that. Aethel is a specialist automotive influencer agency focused exclusively on car and motoring creators — the people who live and breathe cars, whether that's reviews, builds, motorsport, detailing, or the shift to electric.

Our mission is straightforward: fair representation for creators and effective, authentic partnerships for brands. We succeed only when our creators succeed.

What Makes Us Different

Automotive-Only Focus

We don't dilute our attention across every niche. Every conversation, every brief, every creator is rooted in the motoring world.

Direct Representation

Creators work directly with us. Brands get clear points of contact. No unnecessary layers.

Honest Advice

We'll tell you if a partnership isn't the right fit. Long-term trust matters more than short-term deals.

We Only Succeed When Our Creators Do

Our model is aligned with creator growth. When they win, we win.
